About QuickNotes

QuickNotes is a simple notes app for jotting down ideas, reminders and to-dos the moment they come to you. It keeps everything in one short list so you can see what matters at a glance.

How to use QuickNotes

  1. Open the Home page.
  2. Type your idea into the "New note" box.
  3. Click "Add Note" or press Enter to save it.
  4. Check the note count to see your list grow.

Features

  • Single-field note entry for fast capturing
  • A live count of how many notes you have
  • A clean, distraction-free list of notes
  • Works in any modern web browser

Keyboard shortcuts

Shortcut Action
Enter Add the note you typed
Tab Move to the next field or button
Shift + Tab Move back to the previous field or button
Esc Clear the note box
